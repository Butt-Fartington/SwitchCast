# SwitchCast branding

The SwitchCast 0.3.0 controller-and-video mark is an original project asset.
It represents captured gameplay leaving a split-controller display as a series
of video tiles.

The mark intentionally does not reproduce the Nintendo Switch logo, Nintendo's
Joy-Con product drawing, the Google Cast glyph, the AirPlay glyph, or the
SysDVR logo. Nintendo, Joy-Con, Google Cast, Chromecast, AirPlay, and SysDVR
remain the property of their respective owners.

The source artwork and transparent production asset are retained under
`work/assets/`. The Switch Homebrew Menu icon and Settings UI variants are
derived from that asset by `work/make_brand_assets.py`.
